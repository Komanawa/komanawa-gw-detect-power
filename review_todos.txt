Making the connections clear and making them flow logically is important, e.g., nutrient losses from land to freshwater contamination, changes in freshwater contaminant levels to water quality and ecosystem health, the challenges of detecting these changes due to time lags, monitoring costs, and uncertainty, resulting in the need for rapid, robust, and standardized tools, hence the package… Making this logical progression explicit would better justify the development and application of the package.

overall code architecture and design rationale; key modules and files within the package
typical workflows and use cases; expected inputs and outputs (including data formats)
installation procedures and dependencies, and usability considerations for different user groups
visual outputs or screenshots illustrating typical results
These additions would significantly strengthen the paper’s value as a reference for applied researchers and practitioners.
